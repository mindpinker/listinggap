Why it stays free

The analysis engine ships with the extension.

There is no server to pay for, no AI API to meter, and no account to manage. That means no credits, no trial, no license key, and no paywall. The trade-off is deliberately narrow scope: ListingGap does not estimate sales, pull keyword volume, automate PPC, or generate images.

No data uploadReview-page requests go only to the Amazon domain you are already visiting.
No remote codeEvery line of analysis code is packaged with the extension.
No invented factsMissing materials, tests, and certifications are marked as needing input.
No lock-inCopy blocks, export Markdown or CSV, and clear local history at any time.

Privacy

Your listing stays in your browser.

ListingGap has no backend, analytics, telemetry, advertising, account, or remote AI. Listing and review text is processed in the side panel. Up to 20 audits and your settings are stored with chrome.storage.local on your device.

Support

Something not extracting?

Amazon changes its pages often. If a listing does not extract, refresh the product page and try again. If Amazon hides low-star review bodies, use the paste/CSV fallback. For bugs, feature requests, or privacy questions, open an issue.

Before publishing a rewrite

Verify every material, certification, test result, and warranty claim. ListingGap is a hypothesis generator, not a guarantee of sales, ranking, or compliance.
